Privacy Policy

Your devotion is personal, and so is your privacy. Devadwar is designed to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains, in plain language, what we handle, what we never collect, and the rights you have under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Contact for privacy questions: [Grievance Officer name — to be appointed], Grievance Officer, [grievance email — to be confirmed]. See also Grievance Redressal.

2. The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no login.
  • No analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising, no tracking cookies.
  • Your Sankalp never leaves your device.
  • When you begin an experience, our server receives only which deity and which experience you chose. No name, email or phone number is sent.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data for marketing.

3. What we never collect: your Sankalp

You may write a private Sankalp (a wish or prayer) before an experience. It is held only in your browser’s temporary memory while the experience is open. It is never sent to our servers, never saved to your device’s storage, never included in any log or analytics, and never seen by us or anyone else. When the experience ends, or you close or refresh the page, it is discarded. Because we never receive it, we cannot recover it for you either.

4. What we do handle

4.1 Information stored on your own device

Your browser keeps a small amount of information locally. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

  • localStorage — sound preferences: your volume level, whether sound is muted, and whether background music is on or off. This lets the website remember your choice next time. It stays until you clear your browser data.
  • sessionStorage — demo receipt: the deity, the experience chosen, a demo order ID and the amount shown. This lets the experience open correctly after checkout. Your browser removes it automatically when you close the tab.

You can clear both at any time through your browser settings. The website will still work; it will simply forget your sound preferences.

4.2 Information our server receives

When you start a (demo) order, your browser sends our server only two pieces of information: a deity identifier and a package identifier. Our server works out the price from its own list and returns a demo order ID. This request contains no personal details.

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information when your browser connects, such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request. This is used only to keep the service secure and working, and is kept for [retention period — to be confirmed with hosting provider]. We do not use it to identify or profile you.

4.3 When you contact us

If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share, usually your email address or phone number and your message. We use it only to reply to you and resolve your request, and keep it for [retention period — to be confirmed, e.g. up to 12 months after the matter is closed], unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or any third-party tracking technology. We do not embed social media trackers. If this ever changes, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it, with no option pre-selected.

6. Payments

Devadwar currently runs in demo mode. No real payment is taken and no payment details are requested.

When real payments are introduced, they will be processed by a payment aggregator authorised by the Reserve Bank of India (for example, Razorpay). Your card, UPI or bank details will be entered on, and handled by, that payment provider under its own privacy policy and security standards. We will not see or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking credentials. We would receive only what is needed to confirm and reconcile your order, such as a transaction reference, amount and payment status. We will update this policy before live payments begin.

7. Why we process data, and the legal basis

  • To deliver the experience you chose (your request to use the service).
  • To reply to your questions and complaints (your request to us).
  • To keep the website secure and working (a legitimate and necessary use).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax or consumer law record-keeping, once live payments begin.

Where the law requires your consent, we will ask for it clearly and separately, and you can withdraw it as easily as you gave it.

8. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Limited data may be handled by service providers who help us run the website (such as our hosting provider and, in future, our payment provider), only on our instructions. We may disclose information if required by law or a valid order of a court or government authority.

9. Your rights as a Data Principal

Subject to the DPDP Act and its rules, you have the right to:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, where it is no longer needed or you withdraw consent;
  • withdraw any consent you have given;
  • have your grievance addressed by us;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To use any of these rights, write to [grievance email — to be confirmed]. Since we hold so little, there will often be nothing to return, but we will always tell you clearly. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once the relevant provisions are in force.

10. Children

Under the DPDP Act, anyone below 18 years of age is a child. Devadwar does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Children may use the website with the involvement and permission of a parent or lawful guardian. If we ever need to process a child’s personal data (for example, if a child emails us), we will seek verifiable consent from a parent or guardian as the law requires. We do not track, profile or target advertising at children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data without such consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable security practices, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and minimal data collection. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

12. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law, and then delete it. Your Sankalp is never kept at all. Data on your own device stays there until it expires or you clear it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when live payments are introduced or as the DPDP Rules come into force in phases. The “Last updated” date above will change, and for significant changes we will show a clear notice on the website.
