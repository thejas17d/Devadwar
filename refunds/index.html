Refund & Cancellation Policy

DRAFT · DRAFT — This document must be reviewed and approved by a qualified Indian lawyer before commercial launch.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

We want every visit to Devadwar to feel fair and peaceful. This policy explains when you can get a refund, how to ask for one and how long it takes. If you are ever unsure, please just write to us. We would rather help than argue.

1. Demo mode

Devadwar currently runs in demo mode. No real money is charged for any experience, so there is nothing to refund at present. This policy describes how refunds will work once live payments begin. If you see a real charge you believe is linked to Devadwar, please contact us at once.

2. How our experiences are delivered

Every Devadwar experience is digital content that opens in your browser straight after you confirm your purchase. Because it is delivered instantly and is used up as you take part in it, there is no physical product to return.

3. When you are entitled to a refund

You will receive a full refund if:

  • Charged but not delivered: you were charged but the experience did not open for you.
  • Technical failure: a fault on our side prevented the experience from loading or working properly, and we could not fix it for you.
  • Failed transaction: your payment failed or was cancelled, but money was still debited from your account.
  • Duplicate charge: you were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • Wrong amount: you were charged an amount different from the price shown at checkout.

In many of these cases, especially failed or duplicate transactions, the payment provider or your bank may reverse the amount automatically. If it has not arrived within the timeline below, please contact us.

4. When a refund is not usually given

Because the experience is digital and delivered in full the moment it opens, we do not usually offer a refund simply because you changed your mind after the experience has been accessed. This is not meant to be unkind. Once delivered, the content cannot be “returned”.

Our experiences are symbolic and make no promise of any spiritual or other outcome, so a refund cannot be based on whether a wish or prayer has been fulfilled.

That said, if something about your experience genuinely disappointed you, tell us. We look at every request individually and in good faith. Nothing in this policy affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

5. Cancellation

  • Before payment: you can leave checkout at any time before you confirm. Nothing is charged.
  • After payment, before the experience opens: if you were charged but the experience never opened, this is treated as “charged but not delivered” and refunded in full.
  • No cancellation charges: we do not charge any fee to cancel or to process a refund.
  • No subscriptions: each purchase is a single, one-time payment. There is nothing to unsubscribe from and no auto-renewal.

6. How to request a refund

Please email [support email — to be confirmed] within [7 days — placeholder, to be confirmed] of the purchase, with:

  • the order ID shown on your confirmation screen (if you have it);
  • the date and approximate time of the purchase;
  • the deity and experience you chose, and the amount charged;
  • the payment method and transaction reference number from your bank or UPI app;
  • a short description of what went wrong, and a screenshot if it helps.

Please never send your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP or passwords. We will never ask for them. If you are slightly outside the request window, please write to us anyway and we will consider your request fairly.

7. Refund timeline

  • We will acknowledge your request within [2 working days — placeholder].
  • We will review it and tell you our decision, with reasons if we cannot approve it.
  • Approved refunds are made to the original payment method. They usually reach your account within [5–7 working days — placeholder] after approval, depending on your bank or payment provider.

8. If you are not satisfied

If you disagree with our decision, you can raise a formal complaint with our Grievance Officer, who will acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month. Details are on our Grievance Redressal page. You can also find our general contact details on the Contact page.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, especially when live payments begin. The version in force when you made your purchase applies to that purchase. See also our Terms & Conditions.
