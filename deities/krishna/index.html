About Lord Krishna

Krishna's flute is said to have stilled the cattle, the river and the hearts of all who heard it in Vrindavan. Playful as a child, wise as the teacher of the Gita, he is remembered with the peacock feather and the bansuri. Listen, and let the music lead you inward.
