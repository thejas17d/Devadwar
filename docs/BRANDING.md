# Brand name candidates

Change the live name in `src/config/brand.ts` (one place).

> **Nothing below has been checked for trademark or domain availability.** Before choosing, search the Indian trademark register (ipindia.gov.in public search, classes 9, 41, 42, 45), check domains and social handles, and take a trademark attorney's view. Generic devotional words are hard to register on their own and may need a distinctive logo or stylisation.

Scoring (1–5): **Fit** = fit with the gateway / heavenly-world concept · **Say** = ease of pronunciation across India and abroad · **Own** = likely distinctiveness / ownability · **Risk** = 5 means low risk of implying affiliation with a temple or trust.

## Recommendation
1. **Devadwar** — the strongest concept fit and reasonably ownable. Keep it unless the TM search shows a conflict.
2. **Divyalok** — the best alternative if you want something softer and broader.
3. **Antarjyoti** or **Jyotirdwar** — the most distinctive options if ownability becomes the priority.

Avoid any name containing a temple's or deity-shrine's proper name (for example Tirupati, Balaji Darshan, Siddhivinayak or Shirdi). It invites confusion with official services and possible legal notices (see RESEARCH_NOTES §4).
