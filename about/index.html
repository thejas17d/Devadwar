About Us

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Devadwar (देवद्वार, “Gateway of the Divine”) is an independent digital devotional website. It offers a calm, beautiful space where you can spend a quiet moment with your chosen deity, wherever you are and whenever you wish.

We built Devadwar for anyone who would like a few peaceful minutes of devotion in a busy day. That might be someone far from their family temple, someone who cannot travel easily, or someone who simply wants to pause, listen to devotional music and light a lamp in their heart.

What Devadwar is

Devadwar offers short, symbolic digital experiences for each deity. Each one is a piece of digital content that you view and interact with on your own screen:

  • Digital Darshan (₹10) — Enter a dedicated devotional environment and spend a peaceful moment in the presence of your chosen deity.
  • Digital Darshan + Aarti (₹20) — Everything in Digital Darshan, plus a presented digital Aarti sequence with a brass lamp, temple bells and Aarti music.
  • Interactive Digital Aarti (₹30) — Everything in Darshan + Aarti, plus a virtual Aarti lamp you guide around the deity with your own finger or mouse.

Before you begin, you may write a private Sankalp, a personal wish or prayer, if you like. It stays only in your own browser for the duration of the experience. It is never sent to us, never stored and never read by anyone. When the experience ends, it is gone.

There is no time limit. You can stay as long as you like and leave whenever you are ready.

What Devadwar is not

We want to be completely clear about this, because trust matters more to us than anything else:

  • We are not a temple, and we are not affiliated with any temple, trust, math or religious organisation.
  • We do not arrange or perform any puja, seva, archana, homa or other ritual by a priest.
  • We do not offer temple entry, darshan tickets, queue passes or prasad.
  • We do not accept donations or offerings on behalf of any temple or deity.
  • We do not promise or suggest any spiritual, material, health or other outcome from using the website.
  • The price of an experience reflects only the digital features it includes. A higher-priced experience does not carry a greater blessing.

You can read more about this in our Disclaimer and Terms & Conditions.

Our values

Respect

Devotion is personal and sacred. We present every deity with care. We follow widely known traditions where we can, and we avoid anything that could make light of anyone’s faith. If something on the website feels inappropriate to you, please tell us and we will look at it carefully.

Privacy by design

We do not have user accounts. We do not use analytics, tracking scripts or advertising. Your Sankalp never leaves your device. The only things your browser remembers are your sound preferences and, while an experience is open, a small demo receipt. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

No fear, no guilt, no pressure

We will never tell you that something bad will happen if you do not buy an experience. We will never use countdown timers, “auspicious moment ending” messages or guilt-laden buttons to rush you. Devotion should come from the heart, freely and without fear.

Honest pricing

Prices are shown clearly before you choose. There are no hidden charges, no subscriptions and no pre-selected add-ons.

Original and licensed art and music

Every image, illustration and sound on Devadwar is either created for us or used under a proper licence. Our devotional music is composed procedurally: original melodies are generated by our own software in traditional styles, so we are not reusing anyone else’s recordings without permission. Deity names, mantras and prayers come from shared tradition, and we use them with respect.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to take part. For example, the Interactive Digital Aarti also offers a simple tap-to-offer option for anyone who finds dragging the lamp difficult.
